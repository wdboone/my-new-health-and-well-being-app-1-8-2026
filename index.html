Monthly Goals for 2026Strategic roadmap
Break your 33‑pound goal into sustainable, faith‑fueled monthly milestones.
Weight · Nutrition · MovementSustainable change
⚖️
Weight loss targets
2–3 lbs/month▶
  • Lose 2–3 pounds per month for sustainable progress.
  • Total goal: 33 pounds over 11–12 months.
  • Focus on losing body fat while building muscle mass.

Slow, steady progress is more sustainable than crash dieting.

🥦
Healthy eating goals
Daily habits▶
  • Eat 5–6 servings of vegetables daily.
  • Drink at least 64 oz of water per day.
  • Prepare meals at home 5–6 days per week.
  • Limit processed foods and added sugars.
  • Practice portion control at every meal.
💪
Exercise goals
Weekly movement▶
  • Complete 4–5 cardio sessions per week (30–45 minutes each).
  • Strength training 3–4 times per week.
  • Core exercises daily (15–20 minutes).
  • Take 10,000 steps daily.
  • Include 2–3 rest or active recovery days per week.
